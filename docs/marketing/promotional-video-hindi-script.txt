Ai Review by Digital Hammerr
HINDI/HINGLISH PROMOTIONAL VIDEO SCRIPT

TAGLINE
Ab review karna hua aasaan — Ai Review ke saath.

VIDEO FORMAT
- Format: 9:16 vertical video
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920
- Duration: Approximately 65–75 seconds
- Style: Colourful, clean, friendly and energetic

CHARACTERS
1. Aman — A friendly shopkeeper
2. Riya — A customer who is in a hurry
3. Voice-over — An energetic narrator
4. Ai Robot — A small animated helper shown during the phone demonstration

============================================================
SCENE-BY-SCENE SCRIPT
============================================================

SCENE 1 — CUSTOMER ENTERS THE SHOP
Time: 0–4 seconds

Visual:
Show the exterior of a colourful local shop. Riya enters the shop.

Voice-over:
“Shopping complete… lekin review dena?”

On-screen text:
“Ek chhoti si problem…”

Audio:
Begin with light, cheerful background music.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 2 — SHOPPING AND BILLING
Time: 4–10 seconds

Visual:
Show a quick montage of Riya selecting a product, Aman packing it and preparing the bill.

Dialogue:
No dialogue. Use background music and natural shop sounds.

On-screen text:
“Great product ✓”
“Great service ✓”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 3 — SHOPKEEPER REQUESTS A REVIEW
Time: 10–17 seconds

Visual:
Medium shot at the billing counter. Aman hands the package and receipt to Riya.

Aman:
“Thank you, ma’am! Agar aapko service achchi lagi ho, toh ek Google review de dijiye.”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 4 — CUSTOMER REFUSES BECAUSE IT TAKES TIME
Time: 17–23 seconds

Visual:
Riya looks at her watch and begins to leave. She appears to be in a hurry.

Riya:
“Review? Abhi time nahi hai. Google kholo, page dhoondo, phir review type karo… kaafi time lagta hai.”

On-screen text:
“No time for a long review 😓”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 5 — SHOPKEEPER INTRODUCES Ai REVIEW
Time: 23–30 seconds

Visual:
Aman smiles and points towards the colourful QR stand on the counter. Show a close-up of the QR code.

Aman:
“Ab itna sab karne ki zarurat nahi! Bas is QR ko scan kijiye. Ai Review aapki help karega.”

On-screen text:
“Ab review karna hua aasaan”

Audio:
Add a soft transition sound as the QR stand appears.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 6 — STEP 1: SCAN THE QR CODE
Time: 30–36 seconds

Visual:
Show the phone completely straight and clearly visible. Riya scans the QR code. Add a colourful scanning animation.

Voice-over:
“Step 1 — QR scan karo.”

On-screen text:
“STEP 1”
“Scan the QR”

Sound effect:
Use a short scanning beep followed by a success chime.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 7 — STEP 2: Ai CREATES THE REVIEW DRAFT
Time: 36–43 seconds

Visual:
The Ai Robot appears on the phone screen. A genuine-sounding review draft is generated.

Aman:
“Ai kuch seconds mein ek review draft taiyaar karega.”

On-screen text:
“STEP 2”
“Ai creates a draft”

Suggested review shown on the phone:
“Staff kaafi helpful tha aur service smooth rahi. Product choose karne mein bhi properly guide kiya.”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 8 — CUSTOMER SHUFFLES AND EDITS THE REVIEW
Time: 43–51 seconds

Visual:
Riya reads the draft. She taps “New review” to shuffle it and then edits one sentence herself.

Aman:
“Review pasand na aaye toh shuffle karo, aur jo change karna ho, khud edit karo.”

Riya:
“Achcha! Matlab final control mera hai?”

Aman:
“Bilkul! Final control aapka hi hai.”

On-screen text:
“Shuffle ↻”
“Edit ✎”
“Your experience. Your words.”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 9 — STEP 3: CONFIRM, COPY AND OPEN GOOGLE
Time: 51–59 seconds

Visual:
Riya checks the box saying that the draft reflects her genuine experience. She then taps “Copy & open Google.”

Voice-over:
“Apne genuine experience ke according review confirm karo, copy karo aur Google kholo.”

On-screen text:
“STEP 3”
“Copy & open Google”

Sound effect:
Use a light copy sound followed by a smooth transition effect.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 10 — CUSTOMER CHOOSES THE RATING AND POSTS
Time: 59–65 seconds

Visual:
The Google review page appears. Riya chooses her own star rating, pastes the copied review and posts it. After posting, colourful stars animate gently from the phone.

Aman:
“Star rating aap khud choose kijiye… aur review post!”

On-screen text:
“Choose your own rating”
“Paste”
“Post ✓”

Important direction:
Stars should appear only after the customer reaches Google and chooses her own rating.

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 11 — CUSTOMER REACTION
Time: 65–70 seconds

Visual:
Riya smiles and shows the completed screen to Aman.

Riya:
“Wow! Bas itna sa? Review bhi ho gaya aur time bhi waste nahi hua!”

Aman:
“Aur humein mil gaya aapka genuine feedback.”

On-screen text:
“Done in seconds ✨”

------------------------------------------------------------

SCENE 12 — BRAND ENDING AND CALL TO ACTION
Time: 70–75 seconds

Visual:
Show the Ai Robot, a QR graphic and the Digital Hammerr logo on a clean Google-colour background.

Voice-over:
“Customers ke liye simple. Businesses ke liye genuine reviews. Ai Review by Digital Hammerr.”

On-screen text:
“Ab review karna hua aasaan”
“Ai Review ke saath”
“Scan • Edit • Copy • Review”

Final brand credit:
“By Digital Hammerr”

============================================================
PRODUCTION DIRECTIONS
============================================================

1. Keep the phone completely straight and front-facing throughout the demonstration.

2. Keep the complete mobile frame visible and use thin, realistic phone bezels.

3. Use real screen recordings inside the phone instead of static screenshots.

4. Use light, upbeat background music. Make it more energetic after the QR scan.

5. Add subtle sound effects for scanning, generating, shuffling, copying and successful posting.

6. Use Google-inspired blue, red, yellow and green accents throughout the video.

7. Keep every spoken line visible as subtitles because many viewers watch social media videos without sound.

8. Make the Ai Robot friendly, small and supportive. It should never cover important phone content.

9. Show that the customer can read, shuffle and edit the review before continuing.

10. Do not imply that Ai posts the review automatically.

11. Do not show stars before the customer reaches Google.

12. Clearly show that the customer chooses their own star rating.

13. Keep the final screen visible for at least three seconds so viewers can read the brand name and tagline.

END OF SCRIPT
