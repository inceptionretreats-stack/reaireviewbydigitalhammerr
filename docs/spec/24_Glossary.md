# Glossary

- **Business / Tenant:** one independent local business account in V1.
- **Business Owner:** authenticated person who controls a tenant.
- **Public Customer:** anonymous person scanning/opening a business link.
- **AI Review Draft:** editable writing assistance; not a submitted Google review.
- **AI Context:** merchant-provided factual business/service/topic data that may guide wording; not mandatory keywords.
- **Review Mode:** a switchable context emphasis such as Balanced, Food or SEO; never a sentiment/rating gate.
- **Dynamic QR:** stable opaque QR URL resolving through Digital Hammerr, allowing destination/config changes without reprinting.
- **QR Source:** internal label such as Reception or Billing Counter used for attribution.
- **Google Open:** tracked navigation to the configured Google review page. It is not proof of submission.
- **Private Feedback:** message stored only for the business; available to every visitor.
- **Simple CRM:** contact list used only to prepare personal review-request messages in V1.
- **Manual Review Request:** message copied/opened in WhatsApp and sent by the business owner from their own account; not platform automation.
- **Canonical URL:** `review.digitalhammerr.com/{slug}`.
- **Custom Domain:** business-owned hostname mapped to the public Digital Hammerr tenant renderer.
- **Free Quota:** 10 successful public AI generations for a Free business.
- **Pro Annual Allowance:** 2,000 successful public AI review drafts in one paid subscription year;
  owner previews and failed provider calls do not count.
- **Prompt Version:** admin-managed, auditable AI system configuration stored separately from application deployment.
